## What's new

- **A page for every mod.** Click a mod's picture or name in Browse to open its own page: screenshots and videos large,
  its facts and tags beside them, the full description, its files, what Exordium found inside its archive, and Install.
  Back returns to the list where you left it.
- **Videos in the gallery.** YouTube videos from a mod's ModDB page, and videos uploaded with a mod on the Internet
  Archive, play in the screenshot viewer and on the mod's page.
- **Back and forward.** Your mouse's back and forward buttons, Alt+Left and Alt+Right move between the pages you have
  visited: the library, a title, Browse and a mod's page, Settings and Help.
- **Search settings.** A search box at the top of Settings finds any setting across every section, and you can change it
  right in the results. Ctrl+F jumps to it.
- **A new look for Settings and Help.** Both now sit in a centered column with their sections in a floating card beside
  it, like Browse.
