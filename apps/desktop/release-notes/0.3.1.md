## What's new

- **An info card on every mod's page.** A mod's title, facts and source now sit in a card beside its pictures, with each
  of its links (homepage, forum, Discord, videos…) as a row of its own and its page on the source last.
- **Browse remembers your search.** Your search, tag filters, "Hide installed mods" and the sort tab stay as you left
  them when you switch to another tab and come back.

## Fixes

- The version list on a mod's page follows your theme instead of showing a white menu.
- A mod's facts are no longer repeated at the top of its page.
