## What's new

- **Choose how a mod is imported.** Import in the converter now asks whether to link, copy or move the mod into the
  library, with the pros and cons of each. Link is instant and uses no extra space; copy stands on its own; move takes
  the mod out of your Diablo II install.
- **Archive imports are reviewed like the others.** A .zip, .7z or .rar unpacks in the background with its progress in
  the library, then opens in the converter so you can check its exordium.mod.json before it goes in.
- **Clearer progress.** Linking shows how many files are done, and setup says when it is copying the 1.14 core from your
  install or checking the new files, instead of sitting at 100%.
- **The window remembers where it was**, including whether it was maximized.
- **Minimum window size is now 1280×720.**
- Close and Import sit at the top right of the converter card, and the converter selects the first mod whenever its list
  changes.

## Fixes

- Mods imported by linking no longer report their MPQs and DLLs as missing, and character backups of a linked mod
  include its saves.
- The administrator check no longer fails at every start (it logged a PowerShell error and always reported no
  administrator).
