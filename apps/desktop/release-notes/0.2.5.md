## What's new

- **Browse reads mods before you download them.** For zip and rar mods on the Internet Archive, Exordium looks inside
  the archive: its D2SE setup, its `exordium.mod.json` and its own readme. Cards show the game version, language,
  plugins and author, each with where it came from. A 7z mod is judged by its file list, or by the setup in a zip or rar
  copy uploaded beside it.
- **Readmes in any language.** Russian readmes in Windows-1251 or DOS 866 now display correctly. A plugin's readme, a
  log or a data table is never shown as the mod's description.
- **Accurate filters.** Every listed mod is read in the background, a few requests at a time, so the version, language
  and plugin filters count all of them, not only the cards you scrolled past.
- **Browse opens instantly.** Mods already read appear with their full details at once, and are refreshed quietly behind
  the list.
- **Language and contents filters.** Filter by a mod's language, or find entries that are not mods, such as save packs,
  tools or documentation.
- The banner notes that versions, tags and descriptions are inferred and may be wrong.
- Toggling a filter scrolls the list back to the top.
