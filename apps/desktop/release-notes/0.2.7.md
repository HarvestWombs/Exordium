## What's new

- **Discord shows the mod you're playing.** Friends now see "Playing MortalBorne - 1.0.0" instead of Exordium's name,
  with your character below it ("Lv 100 Paladin Hardcore") and the time played. A vanilla game shows as "Playing Diablo
  II".
- **Mod links as Discord buttons.** A mod's first two links (its homepage, forum, Discord…) appear as buttons on your
  activity, for friends to open.

## Fixes

- Characters from Diablo II 1.00 to 1.08 are read correctly, in Discord and in Backups. Their older save format showed
  the wrong class and level.
