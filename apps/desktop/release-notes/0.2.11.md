## What's new

- **New uploads appear on start.** Exordium checks the Internet Archive and ModDB when it opens, so Latest uploads and
  Browse show what was posted while it was closed, without pressing Refresh.
- **Only what changed is fetched.** Starting Exordium and Browse's Refresh fetch just the new and changed entries
  instead of reading everything again, and a mod's details are read again only when its files change.
- **Clear the Browse cache.** Settings › Downloads › Browse shows how much Browse keeps on disk and can clear it, to
  read everything again from scratch.
- **Filters beside the browser.** Browse's filters now float to the left of the mod list, always at hand. The Filters
  button shows or hides them; on narrow windows they open over the list.
- **Hide Latest uploads or Recent from their header.** A ⋮ menu next to each heading hides the section. Turn it back on
  from the library's Filters menu.
